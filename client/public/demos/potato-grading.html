How this line works

  1. Hopper: bulk potatoes are fed at the set feed rate.
  2. Singulator + roller table: keeps a minimum gap so potatoes pass one at a time, and rollers spin them so the camera sees the full surface.
  3. Vision chamber: LED-lit camera measures width (the grading dimension, like a square-mesh sieve), length, shape ratio and estimated weight, and detects defects: bruise, greening, rot, crack, scab, sprout.
  4. Decision logic: any defect → Reject; length/width ratio above limit → Reject (misshapen); width < 35 mm → Reject (undersize); otherwise Large / Medium / Small by the size boundaries.
  5. Air-jet ejectors: fire when the potato reaches the bin for its grade. Small potatoes ride to the belt end.
  6. Accuracy: the simulation knows each potato's true condition, so it compares the camera's decision against the truth. Lower the camera accuracy to see missed defects and false alarms.
Scale: 1 px = 2 mm. Weight is estimated from an ellipsoid volume × 1.08 g/cm³ (typical potato density).
